# Amplora — One-Pager

**Amplora is a commerce platform we license to local newspapers.** Three products run on top: AI Marketing Agent for SMBs, Network-Amplified Display Advertising, and Tier 4 Inventory Verticals. Operated by **Amplify Media Group**.

> *"Your local newspaper is now a commerce platform. We market your business, sell your ads, and put your inventory in front of every shopper in the region — at a price that actually works for a small-town economy."*

---

## Why it sells — the dual-value pitch

**Every Marketing Agent subscription does two things at the same time:**

1. **Gives the small business its own marketing system** — voice-interview onboarding, social posting, Google Business Profile management, review responses, hosted website, dashboard. Something that costs $500–2,000/month at an agency or $30–50k/year for a hire. We deliver it for $99–$499/month because AI does the work.
2. **Pours that business's information into the Amplora chatbot** — hours, services, posts, photos, reviews, products. The chatbot becomes the local discovery layer in the territory.

**One subscription. Two outcomes.** The same dual-value pattern repeats across all three products: every product line feeds the chatbot, the chatbot makes every product line more valuable. That's the flywheel.

**Other benefits:** Trust transfer (50+ years of newspaper credibility — no SaaS competitor can buy that). Two-sided moat (publisher relationships + a chatbot already operating as the local discovery layer). Revenue rescue (claws back marketing dollars going to Mailchimp, AutoTrader, Zillow). AI-discoverability network effect (every Amplora business gets auto-generated `/pricing.md` + `/llms.txt`, cited by ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews/Claude). Stickiness (3+ months in, switching costs are real).

## What the publisher makes

- **Marketing Agent subscriptions:** 50% in year one · 40% on renewals · 60% performance tier (20+ active customers, 80%+ retention). Plus 100% of any incremental display advertising you sell.
- **Display Ad Reach upsells:** 65% to selling publisher · 25% split among receiving publishers (proportional to chatbot impressions delivered) · 10% to platform.
- **Tier 4 Inventory subscriptions:** 50% in year one · 40% on renewals. A Tier 4 Base customer ($799/month) = ~$400/month to publisher. **36-month average tenure** vs. 24 months for marketing agent.

## The Local-First Algorithm — the protection mechanism

Hard-coded into the chatbot: **local results always surface first.** Out-of-territory paid-reach ads or listings appear only when (a) the advertiser paid for reach to the user's location AND (b) local supply doesn't fully answer the query. Distance always disclosed. This is how the network grows without cannibalizing local advertisers — and why publishers can sell network reach in good conscience.
