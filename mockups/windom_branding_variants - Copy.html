Option B — Editorial Masthead

  • Feel: traditional newspaper heritage — nameplate + section nav + double rule
  • Utility bar: date/weather/quick links above (classic top strip)
  • Logo: 72px tall, centered as a masthead (not a small nav logo)
  • Double rule: navy 3px double border under masthead — old-school newspaper
  • Section nav: navy bar with uppercase section links
  • Courthouse: 32% opacity, more prominent (hero has more room to work)
  • Headline: Playfair 3rem — feels like a print nameplate
  • Tagline: italic Playfair — "trusted since 1886" heritage line
  • Best for: readers who expect a newspaper, longform audience, brand heritage
  • Trade-off: chat assistant reads as editorial, not tech-forward

Option C — Split Magazine

  • Feel: modern magazine/premium tech — 55/45 split hero
  • Courthouse at full opacity on the right — photo is the showpiece, no overlay fighting it
  • Left side is dedicated to the chat product: navy gradient + eyebrow + headline + elevated search card
  • Logo sits in a white pill on the navy navbar — keeps brand readable without changing nav color
  • Search card is an elevated white panel with built-in pills inside, feels like a product
  • Photo caption "Cottonwood County Courthouse, Windom" — adds place and authority
  • Responsive: stacks vertically on mobile, photo becomes banner above the chat
  • Best for: showcasing AI chat as the hero product while tying to place
  • Trade-off: more visual weight on photo; mobile stacking means photo eats a lot of first scroll
