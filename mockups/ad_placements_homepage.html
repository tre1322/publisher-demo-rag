Layout 1 — Heritage Respectful (Windom)

  • Slot 1 — Leaderboard (728×90): between masthead and section nav — mirrors traditional print banner ad placement, highest viewability
  • Slot 2 & 3 — Right rail stack (300×250): classic newspaper sidebar — two rectangles stacked, doesn't push stories down
  • Content shifts from 3-col to 2-col + sidebar rail to make room for ads without feeling cramped
  • Total ad load: 3 display units — light, respectful, non-intrusive
  • Revenue note: local CPMs ($8–15) × high viewability = better per-impression yield than national programmatic
  • Trade-off: conservative — leaves revenue on the table but protects reader trust

Layout 2 — Revenue + Community (Windom)

  • 3-column stacked layout: News + Regional | Business Spotlight + Events + Classifieds | Sports — all content visible, nothing lost
  • Left column: News on top, Regional below — keeps the "what happened nearby" feel from before, just stacked vertically
  • Center column: Business Spotlight (ad) → Events → Classifieds — a mix of revenue and community content that reads as a "what's happening" feed
  • Events card: date badges with month/day mini-calendar look — high scannability, drives local engagement
  • Classifieds card: amber border ties it visually to the Business Spotlight — feels like a "marketplace" zone readers will learn to check
  • Right column: Sports stands alone — it has the depth and reader interest to fill its own column
  • Revenue model: Sponsored Answer ($200-500/mo flat); Billboard (CPM $15-22); Business Spotlight (fixed weekly); Classifieds (paid listings); Sticky bar (monthly)
  • Key insight: the center column is the Grand Network monetization engine — Spotlight, Events, and Classifieds are all sellable inventory that feels like content
