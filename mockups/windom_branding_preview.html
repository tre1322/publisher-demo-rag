Design notes

  • Logo: inline SVG recreation — navy "windom"/"com", blue "news", 3D sphere as the dot with subtle gray shadow ellipse
  • Navbar: swapped from dark navy to white so the logo's navy/blue reads crisply (logo looks muddy on dark bg)
  • Network chip: kept "Grand Network" as a soft-blue pill beside the logo — ties to the platform brand
  • Hero photo: courthouse at 14% opacity, slightly desaturated (25%), positioned centered
  • Gradient wash: dual-radial navy overlay keeps the headline and chat box legible; photo is atmospheric, not competing
  • CTA login button: navy pill for strong visual anchor on the right side of the navbar
  • Search box: elevated shadow reads as a floating card over the photo
  • No changes to the three-column news/sports/regional layout — just a color harmonization pass
